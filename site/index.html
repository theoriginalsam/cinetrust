How CineTrust works
  1. Many sources, no single owner. Scores come from TMDB, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Trakt. With 3+ sources, none can make up more than 35% of the final score.
  2. Every review is checked for farming. Copy-paste text, accounts posting in bursts, sudden waves of 1/10 or 10/10 reviews, and ratings that don't match the text all lower a review's trust.
  3. Small samples don't win. Two perfect reviews are pulled toward the typical score until more evidence arrives.
  4. Explainable. Open any movie to see each source's weight and why reviews were down-weighted.
